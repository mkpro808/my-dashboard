Sombrio Dashboard: Privacy

Sombrio Dashboard is a private family dashboard used only by the Sombrio household. It is not offered to the public.

When a family member connects Google Tasks, the dashboard reads and updates that person's Google Tasks lists so they appear on the family's kitchen display. The dashboard also reads the family's Google Calendar feeds to show upcoming events.

This information is used only to show and update the family's own lists, chores and calendar. It is not sold, shared with anyone else, or used for advertising. Access to the dashboard requires a family member's sign-in.

To disconnect, remove "Sombrio Dashboard" from your Google Account's third-party access settings at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact: the account owner listed on the Google sign-in screen.
